Create a complete, professional event website for **“Hacktoberfest Hack Day Mysore × Vector Flow Club”**, hosted by **Vector Flow Club, Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering (VVCE), Mysore**. The website should serve as the official information and registration portal for the event and clearly communicate the event concept, eligibility, challenges, selection process, registration procedure, rules, rewards, schedule, and coordinator contact information. The event is **exclusively for VVCE students**, and participants can form teams across different branches, with exactly **2 participants per team**.

### 1. Header / Hero Section
The website should begin with a strong event header containing:
- VVCE college logo
- Relevant department/club logo
- Hacktoberfest logo
- Event name: **Hacktoberfest Hack Day Mysore × Vector Flow Club**
- A concise tagline highlighting open-source collaboration, AI-assisted development, innovation, and teamwork.
- Event date: **14 October 2026**
- Clear indication that the event is **exclusive to VVCE students**
- Primary CTA: **Register Your Team**
- Secondary CTA: **Explore Challenges**

The hero section should immediately communicate that this is a competitive, team-based hackathon experience conducted in collaboration with Hacktoberfest and organized by Vector Flow Club.

### 2. About the Hackathon
Create a dedicated section explaining what the event is about. Describe it as a hands-on hackathon where VVCE students work in teams of two to solve practical problems, build innovative solutions, and demonstrate their technical skills. Emphasize collaboration, creativity, problem-solving, open-source contribution, and the effective use of modern development tools.

Clearly mention:
- Participation is restricted to VVCE students.
- Teams must contain exactly 2 participants.
- Participants may form teams consisting of students from different branches.
- The event will follow a selection/shortlisting process before final participation.
- The main event will take place on **14 October 2026**.

### 3. Hacktoberfest Integration
Include a dedicated section introducing **Hacktoberfest** and explaining its relevance to the event. Display the official Hacktoberfest branding/logo appropriately and explain that participation requires both team registration for this event and individual registration on the official Hacktoberfest platform.

Clearly state that:
- Both members of every team must independently register for the official Hacktoberfest event.
- Completing only the internal team registration is not sufficient.
- Hacktoberfest registration is mandatory for both participants.

### 4. Domains & Challenges
Create an interactive **Domains & Challenges** section where participants can explore the different technical domains available for the hackathon.

Display each domain as a clickable card. When a participant clicks a domain/problem statement, show:
- Problem statement title
- Detailed problem description
- Expected outcome
- Key requirements
- Suggested technologies/tools, if applicable
- Important constraints
- Expected type of solution/prototype
- Any additional challenge-specific information

The challenge section should be designed so that new domains or problem statements can easily be added or modified later without restructuring the entire website.

### 5. Hackathon Rounds / Selection Process
Clearly explain the complete journey from registration to participation.

#### Round 1 — Team Registration & Evaluation
Every interested participant must form a team of exactly two members and complete the registration process.

The team will be evaluated based on:
- LinkedIn profiles of both participants
- GitHub profiles of both participants
- The team's submitted description
- Relevant technical/project experience
- Overall suitability for the hackathon

The website should clearly explain that registration does not automatically guarantee participation. Teams will be reviewed and shortlisted after the registration period.

#### Round 2 — Shortlisting & Onboarding
After evaluation, selected teams will be shortlisted for the final event.

Shortlisted teams will:
- Receive confirmation of selection
- Be officially onboarded
- Receive the necessary event information/instructions
- Secure their place in the physical event

All registration, evaluation, shortlisting, and onboarding activities should be completed by **10 October 2026**.

### 6. Event Details
Create a dedicated event-information section containing:
- **Event:** Hacktoberfest Hack Day Mysore × Vector Flow Club
- **Date:** 14 October 2026

- **Eligibility:** VVCE students only

Clearly distinguish the registration/selection deadline from the actual hackathon date.

### 7. Registration Process
Create a simple step-by-step registration section explaining exactly how participants should register.

#### Step 1 — Internal Team Registration
One teammate should complete the official event Google Form on behalf of the team.

The Google Form should collect the required information about both participants, including relevant personal, academic, LinkedIn, GitHub, and team information.

#### Step 2 — Hacktoberfest Registration
Both participants must independently register for the official Hacktoberfest event/platform.

Clearly emphasize:

**One Google Form submission per team, but both teammates must individually complete Hacktoberfest registration.**

#### Step 3 — Selection
The organizing team evaluates submitted applications using the provided LinkedIn profiles, GitHub profiles, and team description.

#### Step 4 — Shortlisting
Selected teams are notified and onboarded before the final deadline of **10 October 2026**.

#### Step 5 — Event Participation
Shortlisted teams attend the Hacktoberfest Hack Day Mysore × Vector Flow Club event on **14 October 2026**.

Include a prominent **Register Now** button that directs participants to the official Google Form.

Participants should receive:

- Participation is strictly limited to VVCE students.
- Each team must consist of exactly 2 participants.
- Inter-branch teams are permitted.
- Both team members must independently register for Hacktoberfest.
